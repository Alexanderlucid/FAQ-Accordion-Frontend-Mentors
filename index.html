FAQs

What is Frontend Mentor, and how will it help me?

Frontend Mentor offers realistic coding challenges to help developers improve their frontend coding skills with projects in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It is suitable for all levels and ideal for portfolio building.

is Frontend Mentor free?

Yes, Frontend Mentor has both free and paid options.

  • Free Tier: Gives you access to a solid collection of beginner and intermediate challenges.
  • Premium Tier:Offers more advanced challenges (with Figma designs and more complexity), which you can unlock with a monthly or one-time payment.

Can I use Frontend Mentor projects in my portfolio?

Frontend Mentor encourages you to build and showcase your solutions.

  • Just make sure to credit Frontend Mentor somewhere (e.g., “Challenge by Frontend Mentor” in the footer or project description).
  • You’re also free to customize the designs, improve on them, or make them responsive if they’re not.
Pro tip: If you take a basic challenge and add your own flair (animations, accessibility improvements, extra features), it can stand out even more in your portfolio.

How can I get help if i'm stuck on a project

Here are several ways to get help:

(a) Frontend Mentor Community They have an active Discord server and a forum where you can ask questions and get feedback. You can also share your work and get feedback from other developers.

(b) Look at other solutions Every challenge has a “Solutions” tab where people submit their work. You can browse and learn how others approached it. You can also see the code they wrote and how they solved the challenge. Super helpful for debugging and learning best practices.

(c) Use Dev Tools If the layout’s breaking or something’s off visually, Chrome DevTools or Firefox Inspector can help you see what's going wrong in your CSS or structure.
